About Us

StrangersAdda is a platform where people from around the world can instantly connect with random strangers through text or video chat. Whether you're looking to make new friends, have interesting conversations, or simply pass the time, StrangersAdda makes meeting new people quick and easy.

How it works?

Simply click "Start Chat," and you'll be matched with a random person online. If the conversation isn't for you, click "Next" to instantly connect with someone else. No lengthy sign-up process is required.
